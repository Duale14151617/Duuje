Privacy policy

POLICE HQ. Last updated: 15 November 2026. Applies to people who hold an account.

Who is responsible

The operator named on the About page. Questions or requests about your data: write to duujesuites@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: username, full name, role and unit, and the work email address you choose to verify.
  • Sign-in and security data: password (stored only as a salted hash), authenticator-app secret (stored encrypted), recovery codes (hashed), sign-in times, failed-attempt counts, and the network (IP) address of sign-ins, so we can detect misuse.
  • Audit log: a record of who did what and when inside the system (for example signing in, changing a record, resetting a password). Audit entries cannot be edited.
  • Records entered by staff: personnel, logistics and other administrative records.

Why

To let authorized staff sign in securely, to protect accounts, to keep an accountable record of administrative actions, and to send account emails you asked for (verification codes, password resets, security notices).

Cookies

One session cookie keeps you signed in, plus a security (CSRF) cookie. Both are strictly necessary. There is no advertising, tracking or analytics.

Who processes data for us

  • Render hosts the application (Singapore region).
  • Neon hosts the database (Singapore region).
  • Cloudflare sits in front of the hosting provider and passes traffic to the application.
  • Google delivers account emails from a Gmail sender account.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept while the account exists. The audit log is kept as an accountability record. Encrypted database backups are kept for 30 days.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. Passwords are hashed, sensitive fields are encrypted, accounts lock after repeated failed sign-ins, privileged accounts require multi-factor authentication, and access is limited by role.

Your choices

You can ask the contact above to see, correct or remove your account details, subject to the audit-record and legal retention needs of the organization.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when practices change.
