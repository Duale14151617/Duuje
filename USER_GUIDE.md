# Duuje / ArmyHQ: first sign-in guide / Hagaha marka ugu horreysa ee soo-gelidda

*Give this page to each new user with their username and temporary password, privately. / Boggan u sii isticmaale kasta oo cusub isaga oo ay la socdaan magaca isticmaalaha iyo erayga sirta ah ee kumeelgaarka ah, si gaar ah.*

---

## English

**Your temporary password expires after 7 days. Sign in soon. If it has expired, ask your administrator for a new one.**

1. **Sign in.** Enter your username (like `ali.warsame`, all lowercase) and the temporary password you were given.
2. **Choose your own password.** The system asks you to set a new one straight away. Use at least 12 characters that others cannot guess. Do not reuse a password from another service. Do not share it with anyone, including your administrator.
3. **Set up the authenticator app (if asked).** Some roles must use an authenticator app on a phone. Scan the code shown, then type the 6-digit code from the app each time you sign in. Keep the phone safe.
4. **Verify your email.** Click the **account icon in the top-right corner**, enter your email address, and type the 6-digit code sent to it. This is what lets you use **Forgot your password?** later. Do this on your first day.
5. **My record.** If your administrator linked your personnel record, a **My record** shortcut appears in the menu.
6. **Sign out** when you finish, especially on a shared computer.

**If something goes wrong**
- *Wrong password several times:* the account locks for a while. Wait, then try again, or ask your administrator to unlock it.
- *Forgot your password:* use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page (works only after you verified your email). Otherwise ask your administrator for a reset.
- *Lost your phone / authenticator app:* tell your administrator at once so your sign-in can be reset.
- *You think someone else used your account:* change your password and tell your administrator immediately.

## Soomaali

*Fiiro gaar ah: qoraalkan Af-Soomaaliga waa in uu eegaa qof Soomaali ah oo hore u akhriyo kahor intaan loo qaybin.*

**Erayga sirta ah ee kumeelgaarka ah wuu dhacayaa 7 maalmood kadib. Hadda soo gal. Haddii uu dhacay, weydiiso maamulahaaga mid cusub.**

1. **Soo gal.** Geli magacaaga isticmaale (sida `ali.warsame`, dhammaan xarfo yaryar) iyo erayga sirta ah ee kumeelgaarka ah ee laguu bixiyay.
2. **Dooro eray sir ah oo adiga kuu gaar ah.** Nidaamku wuxuu kuu sheegayaa inaad isla markiiba bedesho. Isticmaal ugu yaraan 12 xaraf oo aan dadka kale u malayn karin. Ha isticmaalin eray sir ah oo aad adeeg kale ku isticmaasho. Qofna ha siin, xitaa maamulahaaga.
3. **Samee app-ka xaqiijinta (haddii lagu weydiiyo).** Door qaar waxay u baahan yihiin app xaqiijin oo taleefan ku jira. Iskaan garee koodhka muuqda, kadibna mar kasta oo aad soo gasho geli koodhka 6-ka lambar ah ee app-ka. Taleefanka ammaan ku hay.
4. **Xaqiiji iimaylkaaga.** Guji **astaanta koontada ee geeska midig ee sare**, geli cinwaanka iimaylkaaga, oo qor koodhka 6-ka lambar ah ee laguu soo diray. Tani waxay kuu oggolaanaysaa inaad dambe isticmaasho **Ma illowday erayga sirta ah?** Tan samee maalinta ugu horreysa.
5. **Diiwaankayga.** Haddii maamuluhu ku xiriiriyay diiwaankaaga shaqaale, menu-ga waxaa ka muuqanaya gaaban-gaar **Diiwaankayga**.
6. **Ka bax** markaad dhammayso, gaar ahaan kombuyuutar la wadaago.

**Haddii wax qaldamaan**
- *Eray sir ah oo khaldan marar badan:* koontada waa la xiraa wakhti. Sug, kadibna isku day mar kale, ama weydiiso maamulahaaga inuu furo.
- *Waan illowday erayga sirta ah:* isticmaal **Ma illowday erayga sirta ah?** boggga soo-gelidda (wuxuu shaqeeyaa kadib markaad xaqiijiso iimaylkaaga). Haddii kale weydiiso maamulahaaga inuu dib kuu dejiyo.
- *Taleefankaagii / app-kii xaqiijinta waa lumay:* isla markiiba u sheeg maamulahaaga si soo-gelidda dib loogu dejiyo.
- *Waxaad u malaynaysaa in qof kale koontadaada isticmaalay:* beddel erayga sirta ah oo isla markiiba u sheeg maamulahaaga.
